FAQ

Common Questions

Do I need a music degree?
No. If you can write music in a DAW and have a feel for melody, harmony, and rhythm — you're ready. Some of the best film composers working today are self-taught. A degree helps, but it's not the barrier people think it is.
Who is this for?
Producers, beatmakers, songwriters, and self-taught musicians who want to break into scoring for film and games. You should be comfortable in a DAW — but you don't need any film or game audio experience. That's what we're here to teach.
When does it launch?
We're targeting a launch in the coming months. Join the waitlist to be first in line — waitlist members get early access and a discounted founding member rate.
How much will it cost?
Final pricing hasn't been set, but waitlist members will get a significant discount as founding members. The program will be priced to be accessible to independent musicians — not a $5,000 masterclass.
Is this just for horror composers?
The principles apply to all genres. My examples lean into horror and thriller because that's my specialty — but the workflows, business strategies, and client acquisition methods work across the board.
What DAW do I need?
Any DAW works. I use Logic Pro, but the concepts translate to Ableton, Pro Tools, Cubase, Reaper — whatever you're comfortable in.
