The Business — High-Level Modules
🎬
Module 01
Film Scoring
High-ticket client work with independent filmmakers. Revenue is project-based and inconsistent by nature, which is why the other tracks exist. The goal is a growing roster of repeat directors who trust you with their vision.
  • IMDb Pro research & cold outreach pipeline
  • Discovery calls → scoping → pricing
  • Composition, revision cycles & stem delivery
  • Genre-specific portfolio reels as proof of work
  • CRM pipeline tracking (research → closed)

Module 02
ScoreSchool
A 6-module, 22-lesson pre-recorded course ("Score Your First Scene") targeting musicians who want to break into film scoring. This is the recurring revenue engine — scalable, not dependent on project-by-project work. YouTube drives long-term organic discovery; Instagram ads drive near-term paid acquisition.
  • Course content creation & production
  • YouTube → organic discovery → email list → course offer
  • Instagram ads + ManyChat → paid acquisition
  • Lead magnet → email list → course offer funnel
  • Payhip storefront & checkout
  • Student feedback loop → course iteration
Payhip MailerLite ManyChat YouTube Instagram
📡
Module 03
Sync & Library
Genre-specific library albums placed with a sync library that has already signed multiple of your albums. Passive royalty income that compounds over time. This is one of two long-term retirement assets — a growing catalog generates royalties indefinitely, scaling with every album added over the next 10–15 years.
  • True crime album (in progress) + future genre albums
  • Broadcast-ready mixes, masters & metadata
  • Relationship management with library contacts
  • Catalog growth strategy: more albums = more placements
  • Long-term vision: compounding royalty income as retirement asset

Module 04
Content & Audience
The engine that feeds both funnels. YouTube is the primary platform (747 subs and growing) — slow growth but the greatest long-term reach, discoverability, and income potential. This is the second long-term retirement asset alongside sync royalties. Instagram supports with short-form and DM-based funnels. Email is the conversion layer.
  • YouTube: long-form scoring breakdowns, tutorials, BTS (primary)
  • Instagram: short-form reels, DM funnels, filmmaker relationships (supporting)
  • Email list growth → 100+ subs milestone
  • Substack + MailerLite audience consolidation
  • ManyChat triggers: "COMPOSER" & "DEMO"
  • Content → lead magnet → email → offer pipeline

Guardrails — Stay focused, stay accountable
Risk Contingencies · Weekly Review · Boundaries
⚠️
Risk & Contingency
If X happens, the fallback is Y.
ScoreSchool launch underperforms or flops
Fallback: Treat soft launch as market research. Survey early buyers for what's missing. Adjust pricing, positioning, or content before scaling ads. Don't pour ad spend into a broken funnel.
No film scoring projects close for an extended stretch
Fallback: Double down on outreach volume (increase to 5–8 contacts/day). Use the freed-up creative time to accelerate sync albums and ScoreSchool content — both generate revenue without clients.
YouTube growth is slower than expected
Fallback: This is a 10-year play — don't abandon it after 6 months. Instead, audit: are titles/thumbnails optimized? Is retention dropping at a specific point? Shift formats, not platforms. Instagram ads carry the short-term acquisition load.
Burnout from balancing Apple + business building
Fallback: Protect composing time above all else. Cut low-ROI activities first (not outreach or content). Reduce upload cadence to weekly before stopping entirely. A slower pace beats a full stop.

Sunday Night Review
30 minutes. Every week. Non-negotiable.
  • Revenue: Any income this week? Which track? Running total toward $5K/mo.
  • Email list: New subscribers this week? Current total vs. quarterly target.
  • YouTube: Videos published? Views, retention rate, new subs this week.
  • Outreach: How many contacts made? Responses? Calls booked?
  • Ad spend: Amount spent vs. leads generated. CAC trending up or down?
  • ScoreSchool: Sales this week? Testimonials collected? Funnel friction spotted?
  • Sync: Progress on current album. On track for deadline?
  • Energy check: Am I burning out? What do I need to cut or protect this week?
  • Top 3 priorities for next week → add to Todoist.
🚫
Not Doing in 2026
Explicit boundaries to protect focus and time.
  • Building a team or hiring before hitting $5K/mo Premature overhead kills solopreneur businesses. Prove the revenue model first, then invest in help.
  • Accepting free or low-budget film projects for "exposure" Exposure doesn't pay bills or compound. Every hour spent on free work is an hour not spent on ScoreSchool, sync albums, YouTube, or paid outreach. Your Berklee degree and Indie Memphis credits are your proof — you don't need to work for free.
